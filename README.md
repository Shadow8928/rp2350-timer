# XIAO-RP2350-Timer
Imagine your trying to study for 1hr but all your electronic timers distract you, or you like to work on your ysws for 2hr each day but not all at once. INtroducing the RP2350-Timer, The timer holds the amount of time you want to work on each of your seperate projects and keeps track of how much time is left. This allows you to know how much time you have left to practice while not being distracting like a phone.
